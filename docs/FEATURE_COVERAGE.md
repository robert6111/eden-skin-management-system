# Feature Coverage

This package includes the modules requested in the chat:

- Dashboard with appointments, walk-ins, revenue, expenses, profit, low stock, staff present/absent and charts.
- Customer profiles, visit history, preferences, notes, payments and loyalty.
- Walk-ins and queue management.
- Appointment booking with staff double-booking prevention.
- Services, categories, packages and automatic product consumption.
- Staff, attendance, commissions and payroll.
- Inventory, suppliers, purchases, adjustments, transfers and movement history.
- POS with services, products, packages, discounts, loyalty, promotions and mixed payments.
- Tanzanian payment methods: Cash, M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Tigo Pesa, HaloPesa, Bank and Card.
- Expenses and financial reports.
- Loyalty, gift cards, promotions and memberships.
- Notification outbox for SMS/WhatsApp/email provider integration.
- Printable receipts with browser Save as PDF support.
- Reports for finance, sales, inventory, staff and customers.
- Audit logs, user roles, permissions, branch management and settings.
- Equipment and maintenance logs.
- Refunds with stock return, commission reversal and loyalty reversal.
- Backup/restore, import/export and document storage.
- Global search and system activity dashboard.

Some integrations are deliberately provider-ready instead of hard-coded: SMS/WhatsApp/email gateways and fiscal printer integrations depend on the business provider credentials.
