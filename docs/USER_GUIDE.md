# Eden Skin — Short User Guide

## Dashboard
Use the Dashboard for today's activity, appointments and business summaries.

## Customers
Open **Customers** to add or update customer details and view customer history. Reuse an existing customer record rather than creating duplicates where possible.

## Appointments
Use **Appointments** for bookings created by staff or received through the public booking page. Confirm, check in, complete, cancel or mark no-show as appropriate.

### Customer booking link
Open **Send Booking Link**, select/enter the customer's WhatsApp number and open WhatsApp with the booking link. Public bookings appear with source `website`.

## Walk-ins and Queue
Use **Walk-ins** for customers without appointments. Use **Queue** to call the next customer and track service progress.

## POS / Sales
1. Open **POS / Sales**.
2. Select the customer, if known.
3. Add services, products or packages.
4. Confirm quantity, staff, price and discount.
5. Record payment method and amount.
6. Complete the sale.
7. Open the sale to verify customer, items, quantities, staff and totals.
8. Print the receipt when required.

**Regular price** is the normal total value of the services in a package. **Package price** is the special amount charged when those services are bought together as the package.

## Services and packages
Keep service prices and durations accurate. For packages, review both regular price and package price before saving.

## Inventory
Use **Inventory**, **Purchases**, **Suppliers**, stock adjustments and stock counts to keep product quantities accurate. Service-product usage can reduce stock automatically when configured.

## Staff
Add employees and maintain their active/inactive status. Give each system user only the permissions needed for their job.

## Reports
Use **Reports** for sales and stock information. Filters can narrow results by dates, branch, customer and sold item. PDF/CSV options are available where shown.

## Notifications
WhatsApp notifications can be opened manually using **Open WhatsApp**. The system does not claim that SMS/WhatsApp/email was automatically delivered unless an external provider is later integrated.

## Backup
At the end of important work periods, open **Backup & Restore**, create a backup and download a copy away from the salon computer.

## Daily closing checklist
- Confirm today's sales and payments.
- Check unpaid balances where applicable.
- Review tomorrow's appointments.
- Confirm important stock levels.
- Create/download a backup.
- Sign out of the system.
